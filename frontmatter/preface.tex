This Thesis is organized as follows:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chapter~\ref{ch:ch1} introduces the background of the Thesis.
    Its last section, Section~\ref{sec:thisthesis}, summarizes the content of the following chapters.

    \item Chapter~\ref{ch:ch2}, adapted and extended from Refs.~\cite{Doe:PRB24, Roe:PRL25, Doe:SP25}, presents the main results.

    \item Chapter~\ref{ch:ch3} closes the Thesis with the conclusions and an outlook, followed by their Spanish translation.

    \item Appendix~\ref{app:example} complements Chapter~\ref{ch:ch1}.
    Appendix~\ref{app:adapted} expands on Chapter~\ref{ch:ch2} and is adapted from Ref.~\cite{Doe:a26}.
\end{itemize}
